🗑️ Delete Your Account

for Labubu Clicker by Maze Studios

We respect your privacy. You have full control over your data and can delete your account and all associated game progress.

✅ Delete your data

Your game progress, achievements, and leaderboard scores are stored in Google Play Games. You can remove all data related to Labubu Clicker directly from your Google account.

🌐 Using a web browser

  1. Go to play.google.com/games/profile.
  2. Click on “Your data” → “Delete Play Games account & data”.
  3. Under “Delete data for individual games”, find Labubu Clicker and click “Delete”.

📱 Using the Google Play app

  1. Open the Google Play app on your device.
  2. Tap your profile icon (top right) → “Settings” → “General” → “Play Games profile”.
  3. Select “Privacy & settings” → “Delete Play Games account & data”.
  4. Under “Delete data for individual games”, find Labubu Clicker and tap “Delete”.

✅ Once you complete these steps, your progress, achievements, and leaderboard scores for Labubu Clicker will be permanently removed from your Google account. You can always start fresh by launching the game again.

What data will be deleted?

  • Achievements & leaderboards – your scores, achievements, and leaderboard entries stored in Google Play Games.
  • Local save file – the encrypted progress file stored on your device (this will be removed when you uninstall the game or clear its app data).

We do not store your personal information (like email or name) on our servers.

How long does it take?

Self‑service deletion is immediate – your data disappears from Google Play Games right after you confirm deletion.

Note: If you only want to remove local data from your device (the encrypted save file), you can simply uninstall the game or clear its storage via your device’s settings. This will not affect your progress stored in Google Play Games – for that, use the instruction above.
